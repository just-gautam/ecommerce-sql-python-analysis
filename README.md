# 🛒 Brazilian E-Commerce (Olist) — SQL + Python Analysis

## 🛠 Tools Used
Python · SQLite · Pandas · Matplotlib · Seaborn

## 📂 Dataset
Olist Brazilian E-Commerce — Kaggle
Download: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

## 🔍 Business Questions Answered
1. How many orders, customers, revenue total?
2. Revenue by product category?
3. Monthly revenue over time?
4. Which payment methods are most used?
5. Review score distribution?
6. Which states order the most?
7. Top 10 sellers by revenue?

## 💡 Key Insights
- Total revenue across the dataset: **$13.63M** from **95,126 orders**
- **health_beauty** is the top-performing product category by revenue, ahead of watches_gifts and bed_bath_table
- **Credit card** is the dominant payment method, accounting for ~77% of total payment value, followed by boleto (~17%)
- Revenue shows a clear seasonal peak around **May**, with a sharp drop-off entering **September–October**
